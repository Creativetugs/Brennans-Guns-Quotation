We do not turn on card checkout for any product until that payment company confirms, in writing, that this shop and that product are allowed.

10. What you own, and customer information

You get full administrator access. You control the domain, the pages, the products, and the finished site. WordPress and WooCommerce have no license fee. Any paid plugin stays under that plugin’s license, and I buy it only after you say yes.

I also keep an administrator login while I host the site, so I can do the monthly care. You can remove that login when you move the site.

To move to another host, I give you a full copy of the site. You point the domain to the new host. I do that once inside the $100, when you ask by email.

The SSL certificate encrypts the form while it is traveling to the website. SSL does not, by itself, make the stored files safe. This is how the files are handled:

  • Who can open them. Only WordPress administrators: Brennan's Guns, and Creative Tugs while we look after the site. The files are not on a public page.
  • Where they sit. On the hosting account, outside the public file list. The hosting company stores that server. The notice email says a form arrived. The attached files are not included in that email.
  • How long. We keep each file until you tell us to delete it. Your own duty to keep firearms records stays with Brennan's Guns. This website is not your official record book.
  • How deletion works. We remove the file from the live site within five business days of your request. A backup can still hold a copy until that backup is replaced on the normal backup cycle.
